# Breakout

W Kavanagh & N Merchant. Summer 2024 (last maintained, Oct 26)

## Controls

A/D to move the paddle right and left.
P to pause.

## Powerups

big/small paddle (blue)
fast/slow ball (fancy yellow)
fire ball (green)

## Gameplay

If your ball hits the bottom of the screen you lose a life.
If a brick hits the bottom of the screen you lose a life.
If a brick hits the player they are stunned and cant move.
If a bullet hits the player they are stunned and cant move.

Clear all the bricks to win!
